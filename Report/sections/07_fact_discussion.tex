\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Practical Challenges}

Vision experiments were straightforward ($\sim$3 min/run, std $<$1\% across 40 runs).
Language challenges included SAE availability gaps (requiring \texttt{fw-medium} with 8x expansion instead of paper's 4x), ambiguous definitions (correlation measure, ``active'' threshold), and memory constraints.
We interpret ``most features'' as $>$50\% achieving $>$0.75 correlation: fw-small (65\%) reproduces the claim, while ts-medium (32\%) and fw-medium (45\%) fall short.

\subsection{Environmental Impact}
\label{sec:environmental}

Total footprint: 0.168 kg CO2 across 204 runs (Table~\ref{tab:environmental}, Appendix~\ref{app:environmental}).

\subsection{Connection to FACT-AI Transparency}

Bilinear MLPs offer intrinsic transparency: eigenvectors are the learned features without auxiliary models.
Low-rank approximation quality depends on both SAE and model training compute (tokens/param), emphasizing the importance of documenting training budgets in interpretability research.
Weight decay reduces effective rank from 38.5 to 15.5 ($2.5\times$ interpretability improvement) at $<$1\% accuracy cost---a favorable trade-off for transparent AI.

\subsection{Communication with Original Authors}

We did not communicate with the original authors; all experiments used publicly available code and pretrained artifacts.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We fully reproduce vision results: weight decay reduces effective rank from 38.5 to 15.5 while maintaining 97--98\% accuracy with interpretable digit patterns.
Language results are partially reproduced: AND-gate circuits confirmed with semantic opposites (cosine similarity $-0.16$), but high rank-2 correlation varies by model (32\%--65\%); only fw-small (65\%) meets our $>$50\% threshold.
The bilinear decomposition methodology is sound; low-rank approximation quality depends on both SAE convergence and model training compute.
Extensions demonstrate transferable geometric circuits across datasets and CP-decomposition achieving 93.8\% accuracy with disentangled factors at 30$\times$ faster training.
Future work should develop quantitative interpretability metrics and investigate CP-decomposition for cross-domain robustness.
