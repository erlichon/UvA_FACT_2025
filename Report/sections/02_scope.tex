\section{Scope of Reproducibility}
\label{sec:scope}

We reproduce Section 4 (Vision) of \citet{pearce2024bilinear}, which demonstrates that bilinear MLPs enable weight-based mechanistic interpretability through eigendecomposition of the interaction tensor.

\subsection{Claims to Verify}

The original paper makes the following testable claims regarding the vision experiments:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Claim 1:} Regularization (input noise + weight decay) induces low-rank structure in the bilinear interaction tensor, as measured by effective rank.
    \item \textbf{Claim 2:} The eigenvectors of the regularized model's interaction tensor correspond to interpretable digit-like patterns.
    \item \textbf{Claim 3:} Without regularization, the eigenspectrum is flat (high effective rank) and eigenvectors show noise-like overfitting patterns.
\end{itemize}
