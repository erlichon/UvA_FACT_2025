\section{Environmental Impact}
\label{app:environmental}

All experiments were tracked using \texttt{codecarbon} with Netherlands grid intensity.
Hardware: Apple M4 (MPS) for all experiments.
Total compute: 1.74 GPU-hours.
Total emissions: 0.169 kg CO2eq ($\approx$ 1 km driving).
